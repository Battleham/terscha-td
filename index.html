Platform Defense Prototype

Skyhold Rampart

Hold the sanctum against winged invaders. Run the battlements, cast spells, deploy defenders, and revive the fallen before the line breaks.

Rally Revive Place ?

Rally Mode

Tap Rally to open or close the defender tray. When it is open, placement mode is on. When it is closed, placement mode is off.

Placement

During prep, placements land instantly on release. During a wave, release starts a 2 second channel from the hero. If you jump, strike, cast, or revive, the summon is interrupted.

Revive

Tap Revive to raise one nearby fallen defender. Hold Revive during prep to revive the whole fallen line for the shown essence cost.

Prep

Tray opens automatically. Place instantly, rebuild the line, and mass-revive if needed.

Wave

Tray closes automatically, but you can open it manually. New defenders take a full channel to join the fight.

Warrior

Tough frontliner with low damage. Best for anchoring the line and buying time.

Archer

Long range support. Less fragile than the mage and ideal for safe sustained fire.

Mage

Fragile mid-range caster with high damage and area-of-effect impact.

Controls

  • AD Move
  • Shift Run
  • W or Space Jump
  • Mouse 1 Arc strike
  • Mouse 2 Cast bolt
  • Mobile Left pad, action buttons, and Rally toggle
  • 123 Select defender
  • F Place defender (instant in prep, 2s cast in wave)
  • Tab Toggle placement mode
  • R Revive one defender, hold all in prep
  • Esc Pause / resume
